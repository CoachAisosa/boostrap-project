At Foresight Tech Intelligence, our Full Stack Developer program runs for 7 months — giving you enough time to master the skills without rushing. Whether you're learning after work, during weekends, or full-time, our flexible structure lets you move at your own pace. You’ll build real-world projects, strengthen your portfolio, and become truly job-ready without feeling overwhelmed or left behind.

Frontend (React.js, Next.js) Craft stunning, mobile-ready websites, modern dashboards, and smart user experiences — just like today’s top tech companies. At Foresight Tech Intelligence, your skills won't just speak for you — your certificate will too. We are a registered and accredited tech academy, and every graduate earns an official certification that proves their expertise to employers. With real-world projects and a recognized certificate in hand, you’ll stand out in the job market and land opportunities faster.
